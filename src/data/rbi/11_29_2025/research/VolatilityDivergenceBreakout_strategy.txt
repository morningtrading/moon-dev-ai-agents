STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilityDivergence Breakout

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
Key Strategy Components:
- Core logic: Long-only, trend-following breakout that is gated by bullish oscillator divergence and confirmed by volatility expansion. The breakout trigger is a close above the upper Keltner Channel; the volatility gate uses Bollinger Band width expansion; the exit is a correction closing below the lower Keltner band.
- Trend filter (optional but recommended): Require higher-timeframe or long-term EMA uptrend to align with trend-following bias.
- Divergence engine: Detect bullish divergence between price and an oscillator (RSI or MACD) across two recent swing lows.
- Volatility regime detection: Use Bollinger Band width to confirm an expansion regime at the time of breakout.

Entry Rules (Long):
1) Trend filter:
   - Price above 200-EMA on the execution timeframe AND 200-EMA slope > 0 (or price above the 50/200-EMA with 50 > 200 for stronger filter).

2) Bullish divergence (within lookback L bars, e.g., L = 30):
   - Identify two consecutive swing lows in price: Low2 (older) and Low1 (newer), where Low1 < Low2 (a lower low).
   - Compute RSI(14) or MACD histogram values at/near those lows (use the bar of lowest price in a ±2-bar window).
   - Bullish divergence condition: Osc(Low1) > Osc(Low2) while Price(Low1) < Price(Low2).
   - Flag remains valid for D bars after detection (e.g., D = 10) to allow a breakout trigger window.

3) Volatility expansion (Bollinger confirmation on the signal bar):
   - Use BB(20, 2). Define BBWidth = UpperBB - LowerBB.
   - Define BBWidthMA = SMA(BBWidth, 20).
   - Require BBWidth > 1.2 × BBWidthMA AND BBWidth rising vs previous bar (BBWidth > BBWidth[1]).
   - Optional percentile gate: BBWidth in top 60th percentile of last 250 bars for added robustness.

4) Breakout trigger (must occur while divergence flag is active):
   - Keltner Channel KC(EMA length = 20, ATR length = 20, multiplier = 1.5–2.0; default 1.8).
   - Entry on close above KC Upper band (Close_t > KC_Upper_t).
   - Optional confirmation: bar closes in top 20% of its range and volume > 20-bar average.

5) Order execution:
   - Place market order on next bar open after a valid breakout close, or use stop order at KC_Upper if you prefer stop entries.

Exit Rules:
Primary exit (strategy-defined correction):
- Full exit when Close < KC_Lower (a clear correction below the channel).

Protective and profit management (recommended overlays):
- Initial stop-loss (at entry):
  - Stop = min(Recent swing low − 0.5×ATR20, KC_Lower − 0.25×ATR20).
- Trailing stop (after price moves +1R):
  - Trail to KC_Mid (the Keltner basis EMA) − 0.25×ATR20 OR use Chandelier Exit (ATR20 × 3 below highest close since entry), whichever is tighter.
- Partial profit take (optional):
  - Scale out 50% at 1.5R; move stop to breakeven. Continue to trail as above.
- Time stop:
  - If trade has not reached +0.5R within 10 bars, exit at market to avoid stagnation.

Risk Management:
- Position sizing:
  - Risk per trade: 0.5–1.0% of account equity.
  - Shares/contracts = (Risk$) / (EntryPrice − InitialStop).
  - Cap position so that notional exposure ≤ 10–20% of equity per single trade in leveraged products.
- Portfolio risk:
  - Max 3 concurrent positions triggered by correlated tickers/indices.
  - Daily max loss stop: cease new entries after −2% equity drawdown in a day.
- Slippage/fees:
  - Include realistic slippage and commissions in backtests; widen triggers by a small buffer (e.g., +0.05% above KC_Upper for entries) on thin markets.

Required Indicators and Settings:
- Keltner Channel:
  - Basis: EMA(20)
  - ATR: 20
  - Multiplier: 1.8 (tune 1.5–2.0 by instrument)
- Bollinger Bands:
  - Period: 20
  - StdDev: 2
  - BBWidth = UpperBB − LowerBB; BBWidthMA = SMA(BBWidth, 20)
- Divergence oscillator (choose one and stick with it per system version):
  - RSI(14) or MACD (12, 26, 9) histogram
  - Swing identification: use pivot lows (e.g., a low with 2 bars on each side with higher lows)
- Trend filter:
  - EMA(200) slope and price relation (optional EMA(50) > EMA(200) cross as additional filter)
- ATR(20):
  - For stops, targets, and trailing logic
- Volume (optional):
  - Average Volume(20) for confirmation

Implementation Notes:
- Only accept breakout if the divergence occurred within the last D bars and before/at the breakout bar; ignore divergences that form after the breakout.
- Avoid duplicate entries: require price to close back inside KC (below KC_Upper) before a new signal can form after a stopped/closed trade.
- Test across multiple timeframes:
  - Intraday: 30–120 min
  - Swing: Daily primary, Weekly for higher-timeframe trend filter
- Instruments:
  - Liquid equities, index futures, liquid FX pairs, liquid crypto majors. Adjust KC multiplier upward for higher-volatility assets.

Parameter Defaults (starting points for optimization):
- KC: EMA(20), ATR(20), Multiplier = 1.8
- BB: 20, 2; BBWidth expansion = 1.2 × BBWidthMA and rising
- Divergence: RSI(14); lookback L = 30 bars; validity window D = 10 bars; pivot left/right = 2
- Stops: Initial = min(swing low − 0.5×ATR20, KC_Lower − 0.25×ATR20); Trail = KC_Mid − 0.25×ATR20
- Risk: 0.75% per trade; partial at 1.5R

Signal Pseudocode (concise):
- If price > EMA200 and slope(EMA200) > 0
- And BullishDivergence(RSI14) detected within last D bars
- And BBWidth > 1.2 × SMA(BBWidth, 20) and BBWidth > BBWidth[1]
- And Close > KC_Upper
=> Go Long next bar; set Initial Stop
- Exit if Close < KC_Lower (or stop/trail hits earlier)

This specification captures the bullish divergence + Keltner breakout gated by Bollinger expansion, with exits on correction below the channel, and adds robust risk controls for practical execution.
