STRATEGY_NAME: Volatility Divergence Breakout

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS:
This strategy combines multiple technical analysis concepts to identify high-probability trend entries during volatility expansion periods. The core approach detects bullish divergence when price makes lower lows while momentum indicators show higher lows, then confirms the reversal with volatility-based breakout signals. The strategy specifically waits for Bollinger Band expansion to indicate increased market volatility, then requires price to break above the Keltner Channel upper boundary for entry confirmation.

REQUIRED INDICATORS:
1. Keltner Channel (20-period EMA with 2.0 ATR bands)
2. Bollinger Bands (20-period, 2 standard deviations)
3. RSI or MACD for divergence detection
4. Price action for swing high/low identification

ENTRY RULES:
1. Identify bullish divergence: Price forms a lower low while RSI/MACD forms a higher low
2. Confirm volatility expansion: Bollinger Bands must be widening (band width increasing)
3. Wait for breakout: Price must close above the upper Keltner Channel
4. Enter long position on the next candle open after all three conditions are met

EXIT RULES:
1. Primary exit: Close position when price closes below the Keltner Channel lower boundary
2. Alternative exit: Use trailing stop based on recent swing lows or ATR multiple
3. Time-based exit: Consider exiting if no significant momentum develops within 5-10 periods

RISK MANAGEMENT:
1. Position sizing based on 1-2% risk per trade
2. Initial stop loss placed below the most recent swing low or divergence point
3. Monitor Bollinger Band contraction as potential early warning for trend exhaustion
4. Avoid trading during low volatility periods when bands are contracting
5. Consider reducing position size if multiple divergence signals occur in quick succession

ADDITIONAL CONSIDERATIONS:
- This strategy works best in trending markets with clear momentum shifts
- Higher timeframe signals (4H/Daily) typically provide more reliable entries
- Combine with volume confirmation for enhanced signal reliability
- Be cautious of false breakouts during high volatility periods
- The strategy may produce fewer signals but aims for higher quality setups with strong risk-reward ratios
